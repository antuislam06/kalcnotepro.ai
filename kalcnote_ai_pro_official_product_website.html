Privacy Policy for KalcNote AI PRO

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Vibe Labs AI ("we," "our," or "us") develops and maintains KalcNote AI PRO (the "Application" or "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

We believe that your personal calculations, notes, and financial figures belong solely to you. KalcNote AI PRO is designed as an offline-first, standalone application: your notes and calculation sheets remain on your device and are never uploaded, stored, or processed on remote servers by Vibe Labs AI.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike conventional productivity and cloud-synced applications, KalcNote AI PRO does not require user registration or personal identification. We do not collect, process, or store:

  • Personal Information: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or demographic data.
  • Calculation & Note Content: Your formulas, text notes, variables, calculations, checklist items, and exported PDFs.
  • Sensitive Device Data: Precise or approximate GPS location, contacts, calendar entries, photos, media files, camera streams, or microphone audio.
  • Authentication Credentials: Passwords, biometric keys, or social media logins.

All calculations, custom variable assignments, and sheet tabs are stored strictly within a secure local database (Room/SQLite) located in your device's private sandbox storage.

3. Information Collected Automatically by Third Parties

To provide live market conversion rates and maintain our free-tier services through advertising, the Application communicates with third-party service providers. These third parties collect technical metadata and device-level identifiers in accordance with their independent privacy policies.

A. Google AdMob (Advertising & Diagnostics)

KalcNote AI PRO displays advertisements via Google AdMob (anchored adaptive banners and startup open ads) on the free tier. Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID, and IP address.
  • Usage & Diagnostic Metrics: Ad impression events, click-through data, session start times, app crashes, and performance analytics.
  • General Device Specs: Operating system version, device model, hardware specifications, and network carrier.

This information is used to serve, personalize, and measure ads, as well as to prevent invalid traffic and ad fraud.

B. Google Play In-App Billing (Subscriptions & Purchases)

When you upgrade to KalcNote Pro or activate an introductory trial, all financial transactions are processed directly by Google Play Billing services.

  • Vibe Labs AI never receives, processes, or stores credit card numbers, debit card details, bank account credentials, or billing addresses.
  • Google Play securely informs our Application of your subscription status (e.g., active, expired, or in-trial) via anonymized purchase tokens so the Application can suppress advertisements and unlock Pro features locally.

4. Internet Permissions & Network Use

KalcNote AI PRO requests the standard Android permissions android.permission.INTERNET and android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. These permissions are utilized exclusively for:

  1. Live Currency & Commodity Rates: Fetching current foreign exchange rates (fiat currencies, cryptocurrency indices, and precious metals) over secure HTTPS connections. Rate data is cached locally to enable offline math.
  2. AdMob Ad Delivery: Requesting and rendering display ads for users on the ad-supported tier.
  3. Google Play License Verification: Confirming active subscription or trial entitlements.

The Application does not transmit your typed expressions or note entries across the network.

5. Third-Party Service Links

The third-party services utilized by KalcNote AI PRO are governed by their respective privacy terms:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob & Partner Policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play In-App Purchases: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Data: All notes, sheets, and cached configuration values reside exclusively on your physical device. You can permanently erase all application data at any time by clearing the Application's storage via Android Settings → Apps → KalcNote AI PRO → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the Application.
  • Third-Party Telemetry: Advertising telemetry and anonymous diagnostic metrics collected by Google AdMob are retained according to Google's standard data retention schedules.

7. Advertising Choices & Opt-Out

You have control over how your device identifiers are used for personalized advertising:

  • Reset Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset / Delete advertising ID.
  • Ad-Free Experience: Users who maintain an active KalcNote Pro subscription disable the AdMob SDK display components entirely.

8. Children’s Privacy

KalcNote AI PRO is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or market to children under the age of 13. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

9. Security

Your local application database is sandboxed by the Android operating system, preventing other applications from accessing your notes. All external network interactions (such as retrieving currency exchange indices) are conducted over industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect updates to the Application, regulatory changes, or modifications to Google Play policies. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
